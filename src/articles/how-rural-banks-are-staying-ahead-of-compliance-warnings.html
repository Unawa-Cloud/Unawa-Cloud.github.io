Rural banks are no longer flying under the radar. With the AMLC and BSP issuing more frequent compliance reminders—and in some cases, public warnings—rural financial institutions are under growing pressure to prove that they are doing customer due diligence properly and consistently.

What’s Changing?

Over the past few years, regulators have emphasized the need for better screening practices—not just against official watchlists, but also negative news or adverse media. This means rural banks and other covered institutions are expected to go beyond basic background checks when onboarding clients and monitoring transactions.

Unfortunately, many still rely on manual searches on Google, logs written by hand or stored in spreadsheets, and ad-hoc screenings done only once or irregularly.

These outdated methods create massive gaps in compliance:

  • They miss news written in local dialects or smaller publications
  • They don’t leave a clear, traceable record for auditors
  • They put your institution at risk of failing an AMLA audit
