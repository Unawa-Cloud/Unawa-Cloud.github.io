Manila, Philippines – June 12, 2024 – Marking a new milestone in digital security, UNAWA launches "Freedom," an initiative to empower businesses with state-of-the-art digital solutions to conquer security threats and drive operational excellence.

SignSecure uniquely integrates facial recognition into its e-signature platform to ensure that the person signing the document is verified at every step, a feature unmatched by any other e-signature platform.

This provides enhanced security, legal enforceability, and seamless, legally binding e-signature transactions for users worldwide.

“SignSecure provides businesses with the confidence to close deals securely and efficiently, achieving true freedom from doubts," says UNAWA CEO Atty. Regina Jacinto-Barrientos.

Part of this groundbreaking initiative is UCheck, an advanced name-screening tool for AML compliance, which was recently showcased at the 67th RBAP Charter Anniversary Symposium at SMX Bacolod.

Rural banks and financial institutions were given an exclusive first look at how UCheck protects against fraud and ensures compliance.

"UCheck empowers businesses to make informed decisions with confidence, offering robust protection against financial crimes and remaining constantly compliant with all regulations," says COO Atty. Gino Jacinto.

He adds that trust is built on a complete understanding of the customer and their background, and UCheck delivers the transparency provided by global sanctions lists, Politically Exposed Person (PEP) checks, Adverse Media checks, and more, ensuring security for the business.

The symposium also featured a significant moment as Atty. Regina Jacinto-Barrientos and Atty. Gino Jacinto engaged in productive discussions with Bacolod City Mayor Albee Benitez, highlighting the importance of advanced digital solutions in promoting local business security and efficiency.

"Celebrating Independence Day reminds us of the value of freedom. At UNAWA, we understand the importance of freeing your business from uncertainties, and our solutions aim to make your business more stable, efficient, prepared, adaptable, and compliant, ensuring it is rock solid against any challenge," concludes Atty. Regina Jacinto-Barrientos.

UNAWA’s 'Freedom' initiative heralds a new era of stability, efficiency, and resilience for businesses in the Philippines, ensuring they can operate with confidence and strength in an ever-changing landscape.
