Why Compliance Solutions Like UCheck Are Essential for Modern Businesses

One of the standout products developed under Vher’s leadership is UCheck, UNAWA’s name-screening tool designed for AML compliance. Given the rise in financial crimes, particularly money laundering, businesses are under increasing scrutiny to verify the identity of their clients and partners. UCheck enables businesses to do this seamlessly, screening names against global sanctions lists and watchlists in real time.

"UCheck isn’t just another compliance tool. It’s a proactive approach to safeguarding businesses from regulatory risks," says Vher.

Built with a user-friendly interface, UCheck empowers businesses to perform AML checks without disrupting daily operations. For sectors like finance, real estate, and law, where compliance is critical, UCheck offers a reliable, efficient solution that mitigates non-compliance risks.

Vher emphasizes that UCheck was designed to provide peace of mind, helping companies maintain compliance without compromising operational efficiency.

How SignSecure Elevates Security in Digital Transactions

In addition to UCheck, UNAWA’s SignSecure platform represents a pioneering approach to secure, legally binding digital transactions. Unlike traditional e-signature solutions, SignSecure leverages UNAWA Face Recognition (UFR) technology, allowing users to authenticate signatures with biometric accuracy. This innovation transforms digital signatures into tamper-proof, legally enforceable transactions that uphold the highest security standards.

"SignSecure uses facial recognition to verify each signer, so businesses can be confident that their digital documents are secure," explains Vher. "This level of security is essential in industries where data integrity is a top priority."

SignSecure’s UFR technology records every aspect of the signing process in a "Manifest," logging critical details that prevent signers from later denying their signatures. This gives businesses a higher level of accountability, which is especially valuable in sectors where contracts carry significant financial or legal weight.
