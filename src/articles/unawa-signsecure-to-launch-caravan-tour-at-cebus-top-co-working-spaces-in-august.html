CEBU CITY, PHILIPPINES — UNAWA, a leading provider of digital transformation solutions in the Philippines, is excited to announce the SignSecure Co-working Caravan tour set to launch this August 7-9, 2023, at top co-working spaces in Cebu and Mandaue City.

With its mission to enhance business credibility and simplify digital engagement, UNAWA offers the revolutionary SignSecure—a platform that guarantees secure and legally binding e-signatures, perfect for agreements, contracts, and other documents. This innovative platform epitomizes UNAWA's commitment to providing flexible, efficient solutions for the modern workplace.

The SignSecure Co-working Caravan aims to bring this digital solution closer to Cebu's thriving community of digital nomads, freelancers, and remote workers. It promises to showcase the benefits of streamlined work processes, enhanced security, compliance, global opportunities access, increased collaboration, networking, and environmental sustainability.

"We believe this initiative significantly benefits our community and the shared goals we strive to achieve," said Atty. Gino Jacinto, UNAWA's COO and Vice President of Operations and Business Development

As part of the event's agenda, attendees can look forward to various activities designed to educate and engage. These include demonstrating and activating SignSecure, exclusive interviews, prize giveaways, and awarding FREE SignSecure Pro accounts to participants. Moreover, the event will be live-streamed, ensuring that those unable to attend in person can participate virtually.

Echoing these sentiments, the Cebu Co-working Space Community has released a manifesto reflecting its shared vision and pledge for growth and prosperity. This collaborative culture champions environmental sustainability and promotes innovation, making partnering with UNAWA an essential step toward their vision of a thriving ecosystem.

"The SignSecure Co-working Caravan not only enhances the services we provide to our members but also aligns with our values and aspirations," the manifesto reads. "Together with UNAWA, we are making dreams a reality and setting the stage for a dynamic future in remote working."

With dates and venues confirmed, the SignSecure Co-working Caravan is set to visit the following co-working spaces in Cebu and Mandaue City:

  • enSpace, Cebu City
  • Workplace Café, Cebu City
  • Sync Work+Study Hub, Cebu City
  • From Here, Cebu City
  • The Workplace Café, Mandaue City
  • The Company, Mandaue City
  • The Green Space, Mandaue City

The Caravan is on its way to shaping the future of work, one digital signature at a time. To be part of this exciting journey, save the dates, and join us in advancing the digital transformation of the Philippine workspace.
