Applications for an Electronic Notarial Commission begin today, August 10, 2026.

To become an ENP, applicants must complete the Electronic Notarial Commission process through theSupreme Court’s Electronic Notary Services (ENS).

The ENP commissioning process

Step 1: Filing of Application

Pay the Php 3,000.00 application fee through the JePS Payment Portal. Create a PJP account and submit your application through the eNotarization Portal with the required documents.

Applicants must also submit an Electronic Notarization Facility (ENF) Provider Certificate from an accredited ENF. Get your certification for FREE through NotarizeIT.

Step 2: Initial Assessment

The ENS will review your application and supporting documents. Once verified, you will receive the schedule for your virtual summary hearing.

Step 3: Virtual Summary Hearing

Attend the virtual summary hearing, where your knowledge of the Rules on Electronic Notarization and the use of Electronic Notarization Facilities (ENFs) will be assessed.

Step 4: Evaluation

The ENS will evaluate your application to determine whether you meet the qualifications.

Step 5: Issuance of Certificate

Qualified applicants will be issued a certificate confirming the electronic notarial commission.

Complete your NotarizeIT registration

You can complete your NotarizeIT registration even before getting your ENP commission.

Provide your professional information, including:

  • Commission number
  • Roll number
  • Tax Identification Number
  • Integrated Bar of the Philippines membership details
  • ULAS Compliance details
  • Professional Tax Receipt details

Upload the following documents: 

  • Professional Tax Receipt
  • IBP Certificate of No Pending Case
  • Passport-size Photo
  • JEPS Proof of Payment (optional)
  • MCLE Certificate (optional)

After completing your registration, it will be reviewed within 3–5 business days.
